# Development and attribution

GapLens was developed with AI assistance for code implementation, documentation,
source review, experimental design and execution. Separate AI agents performed
the internal reviews recorded in the [v2 validation report](v2/VALIDATION.md).
These reviews have not undergone external peer review.

The repository owner reported independently reproducing the v1 checkpoint
`ecf22de604d14e747bf6607d50b62b4870d24fcf`: 58 tests, 54,432 saved prediction rows
and the Ubuntu/Windows CI checks. The v2 implementation, experiments and internal
reviews were performed by AI agents; their execution and reproduction records
are linked from the validation report.

Research methods, software dependencies and datasets are attributed in the
[source ledger](v2/SOURCES.md) and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY.md).
The [MIT license](../LICENSE) governs GapLens code; bundled data retain their
respective source terms.
